For Students

Everything you need to settle your fees without a trip to the Bursary.

Generate an Invoice
Pick a fee category and payment plan to get a unique payment reference, ready to pay against.
Pay With Confidence
Pay externally using your reference. The moment it clears, your invoice is matched and marked paid automatically.
Get Your Receipt Instantly
An official, letterhead receipt is issued the instant payment is confirmed — view, print, or download it as a PDF.
Track Your Balance
See exactly what you owe and what you've paid, broken down by fee category, at any time.
For Administrators

A single system of record for every fee, payment, and receipt on campus.

Manage Students & Fees
Add and update student records and fee structures for every session, department, and faculty.
Resolve Payment Exceptions
Only unmatched or mismatched payments need a manual look — everything else confirms itself.
Search Payment Records
Look up any invoice by student name, registration number, or payment reference in seconds.
Generate Departmental Reports
Produce a payment-status report for any department or level — print it, export it as CSV, or download it as a PDF.
How It Works
1
Generate
Choose a fee category and payment plan to receive a unique payment reference — modelled on the same reference-based approach used at UNN.
2
Pay
Complete payment against that reference through the payment gateway, using any channel it supports.
3
Confirmed — Automatically
The moment payment succeeds, your invoice is matched and marked paid, and an official receipt is issued. No admin action, no waiting.
