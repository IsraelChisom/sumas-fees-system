The Workflow

Three steps, modelled on a real university gateway

The same reference-based approach used at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka — a unique reference per fee, paid externally, matched on arrival.

Step 01
Generate
Pick a level, academic session, fee category, and payment plan to receive a unique payment reference — ready to pay against the moment it's generated.
Step 02
Pay
Complete payment against that exact reference through the payment gateway, using any channel it supports — full payment, or split across two installments.
Step 03
Confirmed — Automatically
The moment payment succeeds, your invoice is matched and marked Paid, and an official, letterhead receipt is issued. No admin action, no waiting.

Student

Why Trust It

Built the way an official record should be

Nothing partially confirms

Marking an invoice Paid and issuing its receipt happen as one transaction — if anything fails, nothing commits. A receipt is never generated any other way.

Mismatches are held, not guessed

A payment whose reference or amount doesn't line up exactly is logged to a review queue rather than marked paid incorrectly — resolved by the Bursary, not assumed.

Ownership is enforced, not assumed

Every invoice and receipt is scoped to the account that generated it. Viewing another student's record by guessing an ID or reference is refused outright.
